# Fairway Notebook

A golf companion for the phone: scorecard, stats, tips at the right moment, course cards and
hole maps for courses within 100 km of Halifax, weather, steps and calories, and a golf lexicon.

Open the site on your phone, then Share > Add to Home Screen. Your rounds stay on your phone.

Course maps are drawn from OpenStreetMap data, © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Course cards
come from each club's published scorecard; check the club's site before you book. Weather by
Open-Meteo. Calorie estimates use MET values from the Compendium of Physical Activities.

## Stride (iPhone step counter)

A native iOS step counter with live walk tracking on a map, Apple Health sync, history and
calorie estimates lives in the `Stride` folder. See `Stride/README.md` for how to install it on
your iPhone.
